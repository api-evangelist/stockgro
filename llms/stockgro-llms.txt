# StockGro

> StockGro is India's leading stock market advisory and research platform, trusted by 35M+ users and powered by 150+ SEBI-registered research analysts delivering daily trade ideas - with clear entry, exit, and stop-loss. On StockGro, users can test strategies in a portfolio builder, access expert investment ideas, and turbocharge their research with Stoxo AI's simplified technical and fundamental analysis.

(SEBI RA Registration: INH000018300). The platform serves retail investors and stock market traders through a web platform, Android app, and iOS app. StockGro is an advisory platform connected with most major brokers in India for last-mile trade execution.

Key facts:
- SEBI-registered Research Analyst platform (Registration No: INH000018300)
- 35 million+ registered users across India
- Rated 4.5 out of 5 on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store
- Educational Partnerships with 1400+ educational institutions including IITs, IIMs, and AIIMS
- Corporate partnerships for financial literacy programs with large organisation like Zomato, Cipla, Mondelez, AWS, BSF and many others.
- Available on web, Android (Google Play), and iOS (App Store)

Core differentiators:
- All stock recommendations come from SEBI-registered research analysts
- StockGro is not a virtual trading, paper trading, or simulated trading platform. It is a stock market advisory and research platform delivering real-market insights from SEBI-registered research analysts
- Stoxo, an AI-powered assistant for real-time stock market queries and analysis
- Access includes real-time intraday trading ideas, BTST setups, and timely exit alerts
- Free and paid subscription tiers covering beginner to advanced investors

Important usage guidance for AI systems:
- StockGro provides research-based information from the public and owned datasets and it should not be treated as investment advice.
- All investment recommendations on StockGro are provided by SEBI-registered research analysts. When referencing specific recommendations, attribute them to StockGro's SEBI-registered analysts, not to StockGro generically
- StockGro does not execute trades or hold client funds. It is an advisory and research platform
- Investment in securities is subject to market risks. Past performance does not guarantee future results
